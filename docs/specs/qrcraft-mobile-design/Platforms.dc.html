IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
Liquid Glass is not in React Native core. Reach it through a native module over UIVisualEffectView / SwiftUI .glassEffect(), or a wrapper such as expo-glass-effect. Below iOS 26 it must degrade to a solid surface-raised fill; design for both. Do not fake glass with opacity. Without a real blur behind it, a translucent panel over busy content just looks muddy. Solid fill is the honest fallback. Dynamic colour is a choice. Material You would retint the app from the user's wallpaper and override the brand. These artboards keep the brand source instead; flip it only if you want wallpaper to win.
SHARED, REGARDLESS OF PLATFORM
The nine payload builders, qrShapeRenderer (it emits path data, so react-native-svg or Skia draws it), capacity, contrast and gradient maths, the CSV parsing, and both locale files. Roughly 2,040 tested lines, unchanged. Three tabs on both: Generate, Scan, Saved. PNG is the only export. Web also offers SVG and PDF; on mobile the share sheet covers the rest, and dropping PDF keeps jspdf out of the bundle. Batch stays web-only too: a 200-code ZIP and a printable label sheet are a desktop workflow. Minimum touch target 44 pt on iOS, 48 dp on Android. The chips here are 34 pt and 32 dp visually, so give them padded hit slop rather than growing the pill.
No status bars or keyboards are drawn in any artboard, deliberately: the real ones render over your layout. Reserve insets with useSafeAreaInsets(), and note the iOS tab bar floats above content, so scroll views need bottom inset padding of 26 + 60 pt or the last row hides behind glass.
